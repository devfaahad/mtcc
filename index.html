About MTCC

Cricket Meets
the Costa del Sol

Malaga Tech Cricket Club is a community club based in Málaga, competing in the Cricket España league. We're a diverse squad — expats, locals, and cricket enthusiasts of every background — united by a love of the game and the sunshine of southern Spain.

The tech community is at our core, but the pitch is open to everyone. Whether you're a seasoned cricketer or someone who's always wanted to pick up a bat, there's a place for you at MTCC.

Get Involved

How to Join

Three steps between you and a place in the team.

Step 01

Come to Training

Drop us a message on Instagram to find out when and where we're training. Come along — no kit required, just yourself and a willingness to play.

Step 02

Meet the Squad

Have a bat, bowl a few balls, get to know the team. We're a friendly bunch — good cricket and good company, every session.

Step 03

Get on the Team Sheet

Ready to commit? We'll get you registered with Cricket España and on the team sheet for the next fixture. Welcome to MTCC.
